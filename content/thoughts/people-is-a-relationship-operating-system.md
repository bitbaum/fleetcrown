## What People Is
People is a relationship operating system.

It is not just an address book. It is the part of the system that keeps social context from evaporating between interactions.

That means it should track more than names:

- relationship health
- channels
- recent interactions
- follow-up context
- relevance to projects and goals

## Why It Matters
Knowledge workers often run rich project systems and poor relationship systems. Important people drift not because they do not matter, but because the operating surface for relationships is too weak.

People exists to give that part of life an actual control surface.

## What Success Looks Like
People succeeds when you can open it and immediately understand:

- who is active
- who is fading
- who needs outreach
- who matters to which ongoing work
