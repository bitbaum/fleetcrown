## What Events Are
Events are the system’s model of upcoming reality.

They capture things whose importance is shaped by time:

- deadlines
- opportunities
- scheduled moments
- external commitments

## Why They Matter
Projects and goals can stay abstract. Events cannot. Events force a date-shaped encounter with reality.

That is exactly why they belong in the system. They create urgency gradients that should influence Today, alerts, and prioritization.

## What Success Looks Like
Events succeed when important future moments stop arriving as surprises.
