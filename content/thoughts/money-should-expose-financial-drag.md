## What Money Is For
Money exists to expose financial drag.

The main target is not full accounting. The target is operational clarity around:

- subscriptions
- recurring burn
- commitment load
- verification state
- upcoming dues

## Why It Matters
Recurring spend is dangerous precisely because it becomes background noise. Once enough of it accumulates, the user loses sight of what is actually consuming attention and budget.

Money turns that invisible drag back into a decision surface.

## What Success Looks Like
Money succeeds when a user can answer three questions immediately:

- what am I paying for
- what is overdue or unverifed
- what monthly burden am I carrying
