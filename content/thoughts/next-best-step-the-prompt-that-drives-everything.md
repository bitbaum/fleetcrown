## What Next Best Step Is
Next Best Step is a prioritization-and-execution prompt intent. It asks for the highest-impact actionable move right now, then executes it fully.

## Decision Order
- Resume interrupted or uncommitted work first
- Fix failing tests or broken flows second
- Address SSOT/DRY/quality violations third
- Resolve mission/product misalignment fourth

## Handoff Format
- done: one sentence of completed work
- next: one sentence of highest-impact remaining work
- tests: pass/fail snapshot
- todos: concise count/state
- health: good / needs attention / critical
